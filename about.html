A Tradition of Precision in South Kolkata

At Bengal Interior, we believe every home should tell a story of calm, order, and durability. Founded on the principle that interior craftsmanship should last decades rather than years, our workshop pairs precision European CNC joinery with seasoned Kolkata carpentering traditions.

Unlike mass-market interior aggregators who outsource work to temporary laborers, our full-time crew of carpenters, polishers, and site supervisors manage every millimeter from initial 3D visualization to final screw tightening.

Pure Materiality

Strict Grade Material Sourcing

We never use cheap particle board or hollow MDF. Every framework is built to withstand Kolkata's monsoon humidity and tropical climate.

BWP IS:710 Marine Ply

72-hour boiling water resistant plywood core for all wet zones, kitchen carcasses, and under-sink cabinets. 100% borer and termite proof.

German Motion Hardware

Partnered with Hafele, Blum, and Hettich for hydraulic soft-close hinges, heavy-duty tandem box drawers, and synchronized slide rails.

Low-VOC Stucco & Quartz

Eco-conscious low-emission paints, durable anti-scratch acrylic laminates, non-porous quartz slabs, and natural teak beadings.

The Bengal Process

5 Steps from Concept to Keys

A structured, transparent roadmap that eliminates contractor confusion and delays.

1

Site Immersion

Comprehensive laser measurements, architectural spatial audit, and lifestyle questionnaire.

2

3D Visualization

Photorealistic 3D renders showing exact color harmony, textures, lighting, and joinery ergonomics.

3

Material Signoff

Physical swatches, laminate sheets, quartz samples, and hardware models verified at our studio.

4

Precision Build

Workshop pre-fabrication followed by disciplined on-site installation with daily photo updates.

5

Turnkey Handover

Deep site cleaning, multi-point snag list clearance, 10-year warranty certificate handover.
